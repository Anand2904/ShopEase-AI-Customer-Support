# ShopEase Product Frequently Asked Questions

## Product Warranty
- Selected electronic products include a 1-year manufacturer warranty.
- Warranty coverage depends on the product.
- Customers should check the product listing for warranty information.

## Product Replacement
- Replacement requests for damaged products must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
- Customers must provide their order ID and photographs.
- Replacement requests are subject to inspection and approval.

## Product Availability
- Product availability is displayed on the product page.
- Out-of-stock products cannot be ordered until they become available again.

## Product Information
- Product specifications are available on individual product pages.
- Customers can contact support if additional information is required.
