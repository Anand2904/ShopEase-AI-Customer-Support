# ShopEase Refund and Return Policy

## Return Eligibility
- Customers can request a return within 30 days of delivery.
- Products must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Certain products may not be eligible for return.

## Refund Process
- Customers must submit a return request with their order ID.
- The returned product will be inspected.
- Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

## Damaged Products
- Customers should report damaged products within 48 hours of delivery.
- Customers should provide photographs of the damaged product.
- The support team will review the request and arrange an eligible replacement or refund.

## Refund Status
- Customers can contact support with their order ID to check refund status.
