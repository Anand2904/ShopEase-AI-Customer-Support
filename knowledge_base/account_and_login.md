
# ShopEase Account and Login Help

## Forgot Password
1. Open the ShopEase login page.
2. Click Forgot Password.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your email for the password reset link.
5. Follow the instructions to create a new password.

## Account Locked
- Accounts may be temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.
- Customers should contact support if they cannot access their accounts.

## Update Email Address
- Customers can request an email address update through customer support.
- Account ownership verification may be required.

## Account Security
- Customers should never share their passwords or OTPs.
- ShopEase support will never ask customers to disclose their passwords.
