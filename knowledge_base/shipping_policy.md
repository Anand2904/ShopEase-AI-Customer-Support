
# ShopEase Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery
- Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.
- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.
- Customers receive a tracking number after dispatch.

## Delayed Orders
- If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery period, customers should contact support.
- Customers must provide their order ID.
- The support team will investigate the delivery status.

## Shipping Charges
- Standard shipping is free for orders above ₹999.
- Orders below ₹999 have a shipping fee of ₹50.

## Lost Packages
- Customers should report packages marked as delivered but not received.
- The support team will investigate with the delivery partner.
